## AETHER//SYNTAX 0.7.1-aether.1

The first build of AETHER//SYNTAX, a music-theory toy, on top of CoralFlower 0.7.1. AETHER opens at power-on; HOME
switches to the CoralFlower synth, and holding HOME there comes back. Controls, modes and the theory are in
[docs/AETHER.md](https://github.com/hvemnd/aether/blob/main/docs/AETHER.md).

Install `coralflower-0.7.1-aether.1.fwsc` from the CoralFlower installer page
(https://ficusficusov.github.io/CoralFlower/): expand **Another version, or a build of your own** and choose this file.
It has not been tested on hardware yet. Keep M-VAVE's stock firmware handy to go back.

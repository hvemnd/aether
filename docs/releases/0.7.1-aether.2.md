## AETHER//SYNTAX 0.7.1-aether.2

**LEARN**, a course of 36 lessons that teaches and quizzes every note, interval, chord, scale and mode, from the first
three white keys to altered chords and symmetric scales. Each new idea is taught (shown, lit on the keys, heard,
played once), then asked with spaced repetition alongside review of the earlier lessons. A tutor keeps track of what
you miss, what you confuse, and what you answer slowly, and its report says what to work on, or which lesson to go
back to. Pick the lesson with ALGORITHM, since nothing is saved over a power-off. AETHER now opens in LEARN.

**13 themes** under GLO → ALGORITHM (saved): REZ, PHOSPHOR, AMBER, VAPOR, BLOOD MOON, ICE, SOLAR, MONO, NEON TOKYO,
ABYSS, PAPER, FOREST, SUNSET.

Details: [docs/AETHER.md](https://github.com/hvemnd/aether/blob/main/docs/AETHER.md). Install
`coralflower-0.7.1-aether.2.fwsc` from https://ficusficusov.github.io/CoralFlower/ under **Another version, or a
build of your own**.
